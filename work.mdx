## Smaller solves

<Card title="One system to check, not two">
  **AnewX**

  <img src="/images/case-studies/anewx-sr75-crew.webp" alt="" className="work-photo" />

  AnewX ran OEM telemetry on some machines and aftermarket trackers on others — two systems nobody
  had time to reconcile day to day. We integrated both into Trackunit and SafetyCulture as a single
  feed, so the operations team has one place to check, not two.
</Card>

<Card title="Making a week of inspections visible at a glance">
  **AnewX**

  Inspection data was piling up in SafetyCulture as free-form entries — useful in the moment,
  invisible afterwards. We pulled it into Postgres and built Tableau dashboards on top, so patterns
  across weeks and sites became visible to whoever's running the operation, not just whoever filled
  out the form.
</Card>
