The only personal information Teralis receives through this site is what you choose to send us
by email.

## Who's responsible for this data

The controller is **Teralis Pty Ltd** (ABN 76 152 898 649), an Australian company based in
Sydney. You can reach us at [hello@teralis.io](mailto:hello@teralis.io).

## What this site collects

Nothing beyond what the hosting platform records to serve the site. Teralis runs no analytics
or tracking scripts here, and sets no advertising cookies.

## Email you send us

If you contact us at [hello@teralis.io](mailto:hello@teralis.io), we hold your name, email
address and the content of your message so we can reply and keep a record of the enquiry.

## Questions or complaints

Contact [hello@teralis.io](mailto:hello@teralis.io) with any question about this data or to make
a complaint.
