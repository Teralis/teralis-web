The only personal information Teralis asks you for is what you choose to send us by email.

## Who's responsible for this data

The controller is **Teralis Pty Ltd** (ABN 76 152 898 649), an Australian company based in
Sydney. You can reach us at [hello@teralis.io](mailto:hello@teralis.io).

## What this site collects

This site is built and hosted by [Mintlify](https://mintlify.com), a company based in the
United States. Mintlify keeps server logs and usage analytics — page views and similar — which
is how we know whether the site works and gets read. Your browser also stores small preferences
locally, such as a dark-mode choice. If you use the search box, what you type goes to Mintlify
to return results, so don't put anything confidential in it.

Teralis has added no Google Analytics, no advertising or social media pixels, and no
third-party tracking scripts. Because Mintlify is based overseas, what it records is handled
outside Australia.

## Email you send us

If you write to us, we hold your name, email address and the content of your message so we can
reply and keep a record of the enquiry.

## Questions or complaints

Contact [hello@teralis.io](mailto:hello@teralis.io) with any question about this data, to ask
for a copy of what we hold, to have it corrected, or to make a complaint.
