## The problem

When field systems get digitised, people entering notes often assume the note will somehow
reach head office. In practice some of it gets missed — nobody's actually reading everything.

## What was built

**Mitti → Postgres → OpenAI → a weekly lead-indicator safety email**, landing with directors at
6am every Monday. Every inspection note, action and timeline item from the week gets read — by
something that doesn't get tired or distracted.
